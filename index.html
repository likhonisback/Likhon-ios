📖LEGITIMATE RECOIL & AIM MANAGEMENT GUIDE
1. Initial Headshot Drag: Execute a light, quick upward drag during the first 1-2 shots to rise from upper chest to head level.
2. Controlled Pull-Down: When sustaining fire past bullet 3, ease your upward stroke and gently pull down by ~10-15% of your initial drag distance to arrest muzzle climb.
3. Button Placement Runway: Set Fire Button Size between 42%–48% placed in the lower third of your screen so your thumb has adequate vertical runway.
4. Spread Cone Reset: When weapon bloom opens wide, tap-release the fire button for 0.2 seconds to reset bullet accuracy before firing again.
